Company rules

  • 2nd Saturday is a holiday; Sundays are non-working.
  • Approved leave uses available CL first. Any excess approved leave becomes LOP × 1.
  • Leave without prior intimation becomes LOP × 5.
  • Each LOP penalty adds 1 or 5 extra working days to the internship target.
  • College exam leave/absence must have prior information recorded.
